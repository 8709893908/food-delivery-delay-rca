Food Delivery Operations — Delivery Time RCA



Problem



Investigated the operational factors associated with food delivery delays using order, demand, partner-capacity and order-complexity data.



Tools



Python | SQL | Power BI | Excel



Key Analysis



Demand pressure vs delivery time

Partner load vs delivery performance

Order complexity vs delivery time

Demand × partner load

Market-level performance

High-volume/high-delay market identification



Key Insight



Delivery time increases as demand pressure and delivery-partner load rise, with larger orders further amplifying delays during high-pressure periods.



Business Recommendation



Implement demand-based capacity planning and prioritize additional delivery capacity in high-volume markets experiencing elevated delivery times.
